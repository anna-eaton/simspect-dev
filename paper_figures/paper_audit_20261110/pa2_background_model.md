## Blockers / hard decisions for Anna

- DECIDE P1: which models the paper describes. Today's text and Fig. 2 describe STT_6.als's location-based protection set (`STT_6.als:326,331`: all of Mem_s, minus the location read by any load with no older unresolved branch). The audit (R-1) found this contradicts STT ("If a load is unsafe, its output is always tainted", yu-2019 p6 §6.2), and the ultimate models replace it with "the memory read of every speculative load", propagation = identity (`proposed/ultimate/STT_6_u.als` [u-4] `hardware_protection_policy[p] = (Loads & speculation_contract_p[p]).inmem & o_p[p]`, [u-5] `{ o }`). EVAL_MANIFEST v3 puts taint defenses on STT_6_u/Recon_6_u and AMuLeT defenses on Footprint_6_u. My recommendation: describe the ultimate models and give the old results a qualifier.
- DECIDE P2: Recon_6.als's reveal clause is a no-op. Its protection set always equals STT_6's (QR2 UNSAT, ALLOY_MODEL_CHANGES.md §B; `Recon_6.als:326-329`), and it reveals transitively over `^op_edges`, while ReCon reveals only direct load pairs at LD2's commit (aimoniotis-2023 §4.3, §5.1). Any results claim that "the ReCon model" encodes the reveal holds only for Recon_6_u.
- DECIDE P3: SPT models treat initial registers as public. `SPT_6_oneLP_v2.als:375` seed `o_p[p] - ((Inreg + Inaddr) - i_p[p].outreg.(rf_p[p]))` (SPT_6_u [u-6] does the same). SPT's paper says "all program data (memory and architectural registers) starts off as tainted" (choudhary-2021 §6, pdftotext l.609-610). Disclose this as a modeling choice, or change the model (owner-gated).
