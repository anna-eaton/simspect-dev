## 1. Reviewer-level assessment

**The job.** The intro must make one argument: simulated Spectre defenses have no way to be tested against their own security guarantees, AMuLeT tests them against contracts that do not match those guarantees, and SimSpect's three parts close that gap, with bugs and efficiency as evidence.
